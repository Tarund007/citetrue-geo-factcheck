  - *Tier 3:* Enterprise Multi-Brand Portal (₹2.5L / $3,000/mo).
- **Execution Roadmap:** 3-Month MVP to integrate into new business pitches; 12-Month roadmap scaling into a white-label client portal.

#### Part 2: Automated Fact-Checking Application
- **Working Implementation:** Built with Python, Streamlit, pdfplumber, and Gemini Flash, integrated with Tavily for real-time web retrieval.
- **Trap Document Benchmark:** Tested against `fixtures/trap_document.pdf`. The engine reliably catches:
  - *Outdated Facts:* Swiggy IPO status (Nov 2024), Zomato Blinkit acquisition price ($568M, not $1.8B), Cashfree founding date (2015, not 2021).
  - *Fabricated Facts:* Apollo Hospitals 120,000 beds, Satya Nadella as PepsiCo CEO.
  - *Verified Facts:* OpenAI founding details, iPhone 16 launch, DLF Cyber City location.
- **Zero-Slop Standard:** Grounded strictly in retrieved web evidence with real-world source citations, clean CSV export, and defensible error handling.
